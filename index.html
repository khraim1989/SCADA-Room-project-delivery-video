The package

Three 40 ft high-cube E-House shelters

Two Control Vanes and one SCADA Room for a major pipeline project in the Eastern Province. SWS carried every discipline under one roof: engineering and GA drawings, structural fabrication, coating, and electrical and power-distribution fit-out.

The SCADA Room was the first to leave the yard. It was presented, crane-lifted, loaded and dispatched on 30 September 2026, 8 days and 16 working hours after GA drawings were issued. The two Control Vanes follow, planned for mid-October 2026.
