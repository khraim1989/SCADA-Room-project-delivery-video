Drop real project photos here (JPG/PNG/WebP). Then:

- `npm run build` — every post, carousel slide, poster and video uses your photos instead of the illustrations.
- `npm run brand-photos` — your photos get the SWS logo frame: `output/branded/*-16x9.jpg` and `*-4x5.jpg`.

Photos are used in filename order (01-…, 02-…). Put your strongest shot first: it becomes the hero.
